# NovaTech Employee Handbook

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Employees have a one-hour lunch break.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation requests longer than five consecutive working days require manager approval. Unused vacation days do not automatically carry over to the next year unless HR grants an exception.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Employees absent for more than three consecutive working days must provide medical documentation.

## Public Holidays
NovaTech observes the public holidays published by the local HR office for each employee's assigned country.

## Probation
New full-time employees complete a three-month probation period unless their employment agreement states otherwise.
