# NovaTech Employee Benefits Guide

## Health Insurance
Eligible full-time employees are enrolled in the company's standard health insurance plan after completing 30 days of employment.

## Dental Coverage
Dental coverage is available to eligible full-time employees as part of the standard benefits package.

## Wellness Allowance
Full-time employees receive an annual wellness allowance of USD 500 for approved fitness, wellbeing, or ergonomic expenses.

## Learning Budget
Each full-time employee receives a professional development budget of USD 1,200 per calendar year. Manager approval is required before reimbursement.

## Dependents
Eligible employees may add qualifying dependents to supported insurance plans subject to the insurer's rules.
