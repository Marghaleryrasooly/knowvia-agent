# NovaTech Remote Work Policy

## Standard Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote-work schedules must be approved by the employee's direct manager.

## Working Location
Employees must normally work from the country in which they are employed. Working from another country for more than 10 consecutive calendar days requires written approval from HR.

## Availability
Remote employees must remain reachable through approved company communication tools during normal working hours.

## Equipment
Company-issued laptops must be used for company work when available. Employees are responsible for keeping company equipment physically secure while working remotely.
