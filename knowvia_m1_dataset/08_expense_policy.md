# NovaTech Business Expense Policy

## Meals
Employees traveling for approved business purposes may claim reasonable meal expenses up to USD 60 per day unless a local policy provides a different limit.

## Hotels
Hotels should normally be booked through the approved company travel platform.

## Ground Transportation
Reasonable taxi, rideshare, rail, and public-transport expenses may be reimbursed when used for approved business travel.

## Receipts
Receipts are required for individual expenses of USD 25 or more.

## Approval
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the expense is incurred.
