# NovaTech IT Access and Security Policy

## Passwords
Company passwords must contain at least 14 characters. Passwords must not be shared with other employees.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for all company accounts that support it.

## Account Access
Employees must use their own assigned accounts. Shared administrative accounts are prohibited unless specifically approved by the IT Security team.

## Device Lock
Company computers must automatically lock after 10 minutes of inactivity.

## Lost Devices
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately.

Note: This policy does not contain guidance about entering confidential company information into public generative AI services.
