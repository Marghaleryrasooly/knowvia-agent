# NovaTech Information Classification Policy

## Public
Information classified as Public may be shared outside the company without special approval.

## Internal
Internal information is intended for NovaTech employees and approved contractors. It should not be publicly distributed.

## Confidential
Confidential information includes non-public financial information, customer data, security details, private source code, and sensitive business plans.

Confidential information may only be shared with people who are authorized to access it and have a legitimate business need.

## Restricted
Restricted information includes authentication secrets, private cryptographic keys, and highly sensitive regulated data. Access must be explicitly authorized.

Note: This document classifies company information but does not define whether any specific generative AI service is approved for handling confidential information.
